## Description (4000 characters max)

**GitLifting: Version Control for Your Gains**

Transform your workout journey with GitLifting, the smart workout tracking app that brings the precision of version control to your fitness goals. Just like developers track code changes, GitLifting helps you track, commit, and push your way to new personal records.

**🎯 Smart Progressive Overload Recommendations**

GitLifting's intelligent progression calculator analyzes your workout history and provides personalized recommendations for when to increase weight, maintain your current load, or deload. No more guessing—let data-driven insights guide your training decisions. The app calculates meaningful weight increments (2.5 lbs minimum) based on your exercise type and current strength levels.

**📊 Complete Workout Management**

• **Workout Programs**: Create custom programs or choose from proven templates (Push Pull Legs, Upper Lower, Bro Split, Full Body)
• **Exercise Library**: Browse 40+ pre-loaded exercises across all muscle groups, or create your own custom exercises
• **Real-Time Tracking**: Log sets, weight, and reps during your workout with instant feedback
• **Workout History**: View detailed metrics from past sessions including volume, average reps, and progression trends
• **Streak Tracking**: Automatically track consecutive workout days with smart notifications to help maintain your momentum

**💪 Key Features**

• **Progressive Overload Intelligence**: Get recommendations based on your actual performance data
• **Volume Metrics**: Track total volume, average reps per set, and pounds per rep for comprehensive analysis
• **Workout Rotation**: Automatic tracking of which workout day comes next in your program
• **Activity Heatmap**: Visualize your workout consistency with a GitHub-style activity calendar
• **No Subscriptions, No Ads**: One-time purchase with all features included—your data stays yours

**🔧 For Developers Who Lift**

GitLifting embraces the coding metaphor naturally:
• "Commit" your sets as you complete them
• "Push" to new personal records
• Track your "repositories" of workout data
• Optional GitHub integration to log workouts as markdown files in your own repository

**🎨 Beautiful, Intuitive Interface**

Built with SwiftUI for a modern, responsive experience. Dark theme optimized for gym environments with clear visual feedback on progression recommendations.

**🔒 Privacy First**

All your workout data is stored locally on your device. No cloud sync required, no accounts needed. Optional GitHub integration is completely opt-in and uses your own repositories.

**📈 Perfect For**

• Bodybuilders following structured programs
• Powerlifters monitoring volume and intensity
• Anyone serious about making consistent gains
• Developers who appreciate the git/coding metaphor

## What's New (4000 characters max)

**Version 1.0 - Welcome to GitLifting!**

We're excited to launch GitLifting, the workout tracking app that brings version control principles to your fitness journey.

**🎯 What's Included:**

• **Smart Progressive Overload Calculator**: Get intelligent recommendations on when to increase weight, maintain, or deload based on your actual workout performance. The calculator uses meaningful increments (minimum 2.5 lbs) and analyzes your last 3+ sessions to provide accurate guidance.

• **Workout Program Templates**: Jumpstart your training with proven templates including Push Pull Legs (3 days), Upper Lower (4 days), Bro Split (5 days), and Full Body (3 days). Or create completely custom programs tailored to your goals.

• **Comprehensive Exercise Library**: Access 40+ pre-loaded exercises across all muscle groups (Chest, Back, Legs, Shoulders, Arms, Core) with intelligent search and category filtering. Create custom exercises for your unique training needs.

• **Real-Time Workout Tracking**: Log sets, weight, and reps during your workout with instant visual feedback. Edit completed sets on the fly, and see progression recommendations in real-time with color-coded banners.

• **Advanced Metrics**: Track total volume, average reps per set, and pounds per rep. Compare current session metrics to previous workouts to see your progress at a glance.

• **Streak Tracking**: Automatically track consecutive workout days with smart notifications to help you maintain your momentum. Visualize your consistency with a GitHub-style activity heatmap.

• **Workout History**: Browse past workouts chronologically with detailed breakdowns of exercises, sets, weights, and reps. Filter by program or workout day to analyze your training patterns.

• **Optional GitHub Integration**: For developers who want to version control their workouts, GitLifting can automatically log your sessions as markdown files in your own GitHub repository. Completely optional and uses your own repositories.

• **Beautiful Dark Theme**: Modern SwiftUI interface optimized for gym environments with clear visual hierarchy and intuitive navigation.

• **Privacy First**: All data stored locally on your device. No cloud accounts required. No subscriptions. No ads. Your workout data belongs to you.
